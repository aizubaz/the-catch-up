Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The Catch Up uses a private app, connected to Google through OAuth, to publish videos to its own YouTube channel. The app is used only by the channel owner.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to The Catch Up's channel, change their privacy settings, and read that channel's own analytics.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share any information about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • It does not sell or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: reach us through The Catch Up on YouTube.
